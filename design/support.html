Frequently asked questions

Answers for everyday life.

Explore common questions about your accounts, cards and online banking.

Accounts & online banking

Where can I check my account balance?

Use online banking to see your available balance and recent account activity.

How do I review recent transactions?

Open your account overview in online banking to review recent deposits and payments.

What should I do if I do not recognise a transaction?

Contact the bank through your usual verified channel or visit a branch as soon as possible. Do not share your password, PIN or one-time code.

Cards & security

How do I keep my card information safe?

Keep your PIN private, check your card activity regularly and avoid sharing card details through unverified messages or websites.

What if my card is lost or stolen?

Contact the bank through a verified service channel immediately to request assistance with your card.

Will the bank ask me for a one-time code?

Never share one-time codes, passwords or PINs with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.
